Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Applies to the Rooted Tales app for Android (com.xenwinx.rootedtales)

Rooted Tales is a reading app for children, made by XenWinx ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app collects, why, who helps us handle it, and how parents can see it or have it deleted. We have kept it as short and plain as we can.

The short version. We collect only what is needed to run an account and remember a child's reading progress. There are no ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and no sale of data. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location. A parent can ask us to delete everything at any time.

1. Children and parents

Rooted Tales is made for children aged 4 to 12 to enjoy with a parent or guardian. Accounts are meant to be created by a parent or guardian, or by a child with a parent's permission and help. We do not ask children for their real name, home address, phone number or location, and we ask parents not to put that kind of information into usernames or profile fields.

If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent's permission, please email hub@xenwinx.com and we will delete it.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the app: signing in, saving and syncing progress, delivering purchases, sending account emails such as confirmation and password reset, answering support requests, and keeping the service secure. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or marketing to children, and we do not sell or rent it.

5. Security

All data moves between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each signed-in account can read and change only its own record. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information and will notify affected families if a breach occurs, as the law requires.

6. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep account information for as long as the account exists. When a parent asks us to delete the account, we delete the sign-in account and the profile, progress, purchase records and photo linked to it. Full steps are on the account deletion page. Deleted data may remain in routine encrypted backups for a short time (up to 30 days) before being overwritten.

7. Parents' choices

A parent or guardian can at any time: ask what information we hold about their child; correct it; ask us to stop collecting more; or ask us to delete the account and its data. Write to hub@xenwinx.com from the email address on the account. We reply within 30 days.

8. Device permissions

The app needs internet access to sign in, sync and load stories, may use network state to detect when you are offline, may use vibration for feedback, and uses Google Play for purchases. If a parent uploads a profile photo, Android's own picker is used and the app sees only the one picture that was chosen.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we will tell parents in the app or by email before it takes effect. If we ever add advertising, we will update this policy first and name the partner.
